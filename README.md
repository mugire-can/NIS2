Practical case study: bringing HydroRégie, a public drinking water utility
serving 450,000 inhabitants and qualified as an *essential entity*, into
compliance with Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2).

## Executive summary

HydroRégie was confirmed as an **essential entity** under NIS2 (Annex I,
drinking water sector). Its current IT and OT networks are not segmented,
which was identified as the single highest-priority gap against the ten
Article 21 risk-management measures. This project delivers, in four blocks:
a regulatory qualification, a prioritized gap-analysis roadmap, a segmented
IT/OT network architecture that closes the top-priority gap, and a
crisis-management chronology with ready-to-use Article 23 regulatory
notification drafts (24h / 72h / final report). Together they take
HydroRégie from an unstructured starting position to a documented,
defensible NIS2 compliance program.
